## Lab Notebook Format

Every research experiment follows a reproducible scientific and engineering structure:
- **Question:** The fundamental technical uncertainty or constraint being investigated.
- **Hypothesis:** Testable expectation before conducting experiments.
- **Motivation:** Practical engineering requirement driving the investigation.
- **Method:** Specific benchmark procedures and simulation parameters.
- **Tools:** Hardware, firmware, libraries, or profiling instrumentation.
- **Observations:** Empirical data recorded during testing.
- **Results:** Confirmed outcomes and measurable benchmarks.
- **Lessons:** Core engineering takeaways and anti-patterns identified.
- **Future Questions:** Ongoing inquiries generated by the findings.

## Adding Research Entries

To document a new experiment:
1. Open `src/data/portfolioData.ts`.
2. Add a new `ResearchExperiment` entry to `RESEARCH_EXPERIMENTS`.
3. Set the appropriate status (`Active Investigation`, `Concept Phase`, `Benchmarking`, or `Documented`).
